## 12. Ranked top ten items for the tutor

Ranked by how much of the tutor each item unlocks against how hard it is to obtain legally. The first five can be loaded or coded now without asking anyone, the next five need one email each. Sections are given in parentheses.

| Rank | Item | Why it ranks here | Licence step | First action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pavlou 2018, *Morphosyntactic Dependencies and Verb Movement in Cypriot Greek*, with its appendices on verb morphology and verb position (section 1) | The only full description of the verbal paradigms (active and nonactive, suppletive stems, participles) plus verb position and clitic order, explicit enough to code as paradigm tables and placement rules | None for the rules. The appendix tables and example sentences are Ask (author copyright), so the paradigms must be re-generated from the stated rules with the tutor's own lexemes | Transcribe the inflection classes into a paradigm generator and test it against the Wiktionary inflection tables |
| 2 | The clitic placement rule set: Agouraki 2001 and Chatzikyriakidis 2012 for the triggers, Liapis 2007 for έντζε, Papanicola and Armostis 2021, Grohmann et al. 2017 and 2021 for the variation rates (sections 1, 7, 8) | Clitic placement is the most salient CG grammatical feature, the best studied, and the one where sections 1, 5, 7 and 8 all agree on the drill (Είδες το, Εν το είδες). The variation papers supply the tolerance thresholds the feedback needs | None for the rules, Ask for any example sentence reused verbatim | Code enclisis as default, proclisis under complementizer, negation (εν), wh and other filled C, exclude έντζε, and set tolerance for SMG-like proclisis from the corpus frequencies |
| 3 | Wiktionary, Κατηγορία:Κυπριακά on Βικιλεξικό (about 490 pages) and Category:Cypriot Greek on English Wiktionary (64 pages), CC BY-SA 4.0, XML dumps and API (section 2) | The only open machine-readable CG lexicon, with definitions, some inflection tables and sourced etymologies, loadable today | Attribution and share-alike on the lexicon layer | Parse the dump into the lookup table, record which entries carry inflection tables, map the Wiktionary spelling to the house convention (section 6) |
| 4 | The public-domain verse: Μιχαηλίδης (Βικιθήκη, d. 1917) and Λιπέρτης, *Τζιυπριώτικα τραούδκια* τόμ. Α΄ to Γ΄ on Ανέμη (d. 1937), plus Σάθας 1877 for the Ασσίζες (section 3) | The only sizeable dialect texts that are free of rights, already digitised, and canonical enough that every dictionary cites them. Reading drills, dictation and spelling-variant tests can be built on them without any permission | None. Faithful scans of PD works carry no new right under the DSM Directive Art. 14 | Pull the Wikisource text, OCR the Ανέμη PDFs, normalise historical spelling to the house convention, keep the original as a parallel column |
| 5 | The speech stack: Common Voice Spontaneous Speech el-CY (CC0, about 10 hours, 10 speakers, no re-hosting), Myned AI parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-greek (ASR, 26 percent WER on Cypriot speakers, CC BY 4.0 unverified) and pocket-tts-greek (two Cypriot voices, CC BY 4.0 unverified) (sections 4, 9) | Together they give listening material, learner speech recognition and spoken prompts, all under open licences, the first time this has been possible for CG. Small speaker pool and unverified cards are the risks | CC0 for the audio (use, do not redistribute), CC BY 4.0 for the models once the cards are opened and confirmed | Download all three, confirm licences on the cards, measure the TTS on geminates, affricates and final ν before relying on it |
| 6 | The Συντυχιές orthography convention: Armosti et al. 2014, Αρμοστής et al. 2016 on synizesis, Αρμοστής 2017 on the etymological apostrophe, Armostis 2022, plus the xkeyboard-config code points (section 6) | The only argued, speaker-tested spelling system for CG, and the convention under which the largest modern lemma list (Συντυχιές, LEXcy) is written. Every text and lexicon above has to be normalised to one convention, and this is the only candidate | None for the rules (facts usable) | Write the normalisation table (plain, Greeklish, Wikipedia caron, Wikisource digraphs, Wiktionary) to and from the convention, decide the εν, έν, έν' house rule |
| 7 | LEXcy at lex-cy.com, successor of the Συντυχιές database (over 15,000 lemmas, examples, usage-based orthography), open with attribution but no bulk download, with the Access file lexicon.mdb held by the UCY team (sections 2, 6) | The largest modern-usage CG lemma list with examples and the canonical spelling, ten times the Wiktionary coverage. The code repository confirms the database is not public, so an export has to be requested | Open with attribution per the site, but ask for the export rather than scraping, and ask for the pronunciation audio and the IPA rules at the same time | Email Μ. Κατσογιάννου and cyslang@algolysis.com for an export of LEXcy and the Συντυχιές database |
| 8 | ΚΟΜΟΛεξ, the Cypriot morphological dictionary (about 7,000 lemmas, 5,000 nouns and 2,000 adjectives, with inflection classes), Anastasiadou, Kiourti and Mitsiaki 2017 (section 2) | The only CG resource with explicit nominal inflection paradigms, the nominal counterpart of Pavlou's verbal paradigms, essential for declension drills. On request, no licence stated | Ask the authors for the data and a licence | Email Μ. Μητσιάκη through the project site, ask for format, size and terms |
| 9 | hb20007 greek-dialect-classifier (MIT, 748 labelled sentences, naive Bayes at 0.96) together with GRDD and GRDD+ already in hand, and DialectBench as the evaluation scaffold (sections 3, 9) | A CG versus SMG detector for learner input can be trained in minutes from open data the author holds, and it also serves as the register tagger that section 11's decisions require. | 10 | The teaching layer: the ΠΙ Οδηγός Διαχείρισης Κοινωνικοπολιτισμικής Ετερότητας 2018 (explicit CG versus SMG contrast activities), Yiakoumetti 2005 to 2007 (explicit contrast improves outcomes), Πισσούριος 2019 *Μαθθαίννω Κυπριακά* (the only CG textbook, syllabus model) and Cyprusays 2025 (the only app, competitor scope) (sections 5, 8) | No CG-as-target syllabus exists in reusable form, so the tutor's syllabus must be designed. These four items give the evidence base for contrastive feedback, a graded chapter sequence to imitate, and the competitor's level mapping to beat | Facts and design usable, activities, dialogues and recordings not | Buy the textbook, subscribe to the app for a month, extract the contrast list from the Οδηγός, and design the syllabus from sections 1 and 2 |

Close behind: Multi-CAST Cypriot Greek (CC BY 4.0, three glossed folk tales, section 3) as parsed example sentences, Γιαγκουλλής 2014 *Θησαυρός* (24,054 words, print) as the licensed lookup dictionary if the author supplies files, the ΚΕΕ oral archive (7,046 cassettes, section 4) as the long-term basilectal audio source, and the open phonetic studies of Themistocleous 2017, Eftychiou 2010 and Armostis 2011 (sections 1, 4) for pronunciation targets.

Not found: any single resource that would replace items 1, 7 or 8, that is, a published reference grammar of the koine or a released machine-readable CG lexicon with paradigms.
