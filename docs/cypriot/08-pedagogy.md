## 8. Pedagogy and acquisition

Classroom interventions (Yiakoumetti 2005 to 2007, Tsiplakou, Ioannidou and Hadjioannou 2018) show that explicit contrast between Cypriot Greek and Standard Modern Greek improves pupils' standard Greek and their attitudes, the Cyprus Acquisition Team documents object clitic placement as the core acquisition contrast, and the attitude literature (Cyprus and London) shows that basilectal forms carry stigma even among dialect speakers. No academic pedagogy or CALL study for Cypriot Greek as a second language was found, but two diaspora-built apps (Cyprusays 2025, Cylingo 2026) are the tutor's closest precedents and must be examined before design.
