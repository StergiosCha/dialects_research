## 8. Pedagogy and acquisition

Classroom interventions (Yiakoumetti 2005 to 2007) show that explicit contrast between Cypriot Greek and Standard Modern Greek improves pupils' standard Greek and their attitudes, the Cyprus Acquisition Team documents object clitic placement as the core acquisition contrast, and the attitude literature shows that basilectal forms carry stigma even among dialect speakers. No pedagogy for Cypriot Greek as a second language and no CALL system for any Greek dialect was found, so the tutor's contrastive feedback design must be derived from the SMG-learning studies in reverse.
