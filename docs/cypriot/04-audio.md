## 4. Audio and speech

The only openly licensed Cypriot Greek speech data is the Mozilla Common Voice Spontaneous Speech el-CY set (about 11 hours, 10 speakers, CC0-1.0 with a Data Collective clause against re-hosting), released standalone up to version 3.0 (March 2026) and used by the DialRes 2026 Whisper baseline (best WER 33.93%) and by Myned AI's Parakeet ASR and Pocket TTS Greek (CC BY 4.0, two Cypriot voices), which together are the first speech models a tutor could run. The large holdings (ΚΕΕ oral archive, 7,046 cassettes, and the ΡΙΚ archive, about 500 hours on Ψηφιακός Ηρόδοτος for personal viewing only) are institutional, paid or permission-only, and no checked phonetic corpus has been released.
