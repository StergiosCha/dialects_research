## 3. Text corpora and texts

The legally clean core for the tutor is the dialect verse of Βασίλης Μιχαηλίδης (d. 1917, public domain since 1 January 1988) and Δημήτρης Λιπέρτης (d. 1937, public domain since 1 January 2008), both digitised, plus the medieval chronicles and the Ασσίζες, whose Dawkins and Σάθας editions are also out of copyright (Dawkins d. 1955, public domain in the EU since 1 January 2026). The only open machine-readable corpora found are small: the MIT-licensed hb20007 social-media sample (429 CG lines, about 7,100 words, verified), Multi-CAST Cypriot Greek (three folk tales, CC BY 4.0) and the Common Voice Spontaneous Speech release (about 10 hours, CC0), while modern dialect literature (Λιασίδης protected to end 2055, Μόντης to end 2074) and every ΚΕΕ folk collection need permission.
