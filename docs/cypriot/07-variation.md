## 7. Variation

The literature converges on a pancypriot koine based on Mesaoria and Nicosia speech, mid-continuum and gaining prestige, which supports the koine as the tutor's default register with basilectal regional forms taught as marked (χωρκάτικα) options. No dialect atlas, no regional monograph and no released variation dataset were found, so regional labels have to come from Newton 1972 and the perceptual studies below.
