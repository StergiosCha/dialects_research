## 5. Teaching materials and curricula

One print textbook of Cypriot Greek as a target language exists (Πισσούριος 2019, *Μαθθαίννω Κυπριακά*, first part only, out of stock) and one commercial app (Cyprusays, launched October 2025), everything else teaches Standard Modern Greek and uses the dialect only for awareness or contrast. State curricula in Cyprus and in the UK diaspora schools frame the dialect as a resource for learning the standard, so the tutor's syllabus has to be built from the grammar and lexicon sections, not borrowed.
