## 6. Orthography

No standard spelling exists. The Συντυχιές team (Κατσογιάννου, Αρμοστής, Χριστοδούλου, Θεμιστοκλέους) published the most explicit convention (geminates written, caron over σ, ζ, ξ, ψ for postalveolars, final ν kept), but every open data source found (Common Voice el-CY, the myned-ai ASR model card, social-media corpora, Greeklish chat) writes plain Greek letters or Roman script, so the tutor needs a normalisation table between the convention and the raw data. No page found states an accent rule for εν versus έν, the documented device for the negator is Αρμοστής's etymological apostrophe (έν' from ένι), which the myned-ai card and Βικιπαίδεια also use.
