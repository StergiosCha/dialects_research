## 6. Orthography

No standard spelling exists. The Συντυχιές team (Κατσογιάννου, Αρμοστής, Χριστοδούλου, Θεμιστοκλέους) published the most explicit convention (geminates written, caron over σ, ζ, ξ, ψ for postalveolars, final ν kept), but every open data source found (Common Voice el-CY, the myned-ai ASR model card, social-media corpora, Greeklish chat) writes plain Greek letters or Roman script, so the tutor needs a normalisation table between the convention and the raw data, and no page found documents an accent rule for εν versus έν.
