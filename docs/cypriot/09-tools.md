## 9. Existing tools

The only openly licensed and fully inspected Cypriot Greek tool is the hb20007 text classifier (MIT, 748 labelled sentences, multinomial naive Bayes at 0.96 accuracy), which also supplies the Greek subset of DialectBench. Speech recognition fine-tunes (Whisper in Stamou et al. 2026, a Parakeet model on Hugging Face), one Greek to Cypriot LLM conversion model and an experimental Orthos spell-check dictionary exist but could not be opened, and no treebank, morphological analyser or Cypriot TTS system was found.
