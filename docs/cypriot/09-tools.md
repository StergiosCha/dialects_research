## 9. Existing tools

The openly licensed and fully inspected Cypriot Greek tools are the hb20007 text classifier (MIT, 748 labelled sentences, multinomial naive Bayes at 0.96 accuracy), which also supplies the Greek subset of DialectBench, and Themistocleous's GPL nlp_editor with a CG orthography to IPA converter. Speech recognition fine-tunes (Whisper in Stamou et al. 2026, a Parakeet model on Hugging Face), Myned AI's Pocket TTS Greek (listed in the Kyutai README, Cypriot voices per its card), one Greek to Cypriot LLM conversion model and an experimental Orthos spell-check dictionary exist but could not be opened, and no treebank, morphological analyser or Cypriot text normaliser was found.
