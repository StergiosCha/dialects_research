## 2. Lexical resources

The largest Kozani dictionary is Χριστόδουλος Χριστοδούλου, *Κοζανιτολόγιο: Λεξικό του Κοζανίτικου Ιδιώματος* (2017, about 6,600 lemmas per press reports), followed by the glossary volume of Κώστας Ντίνας, *Το γλωσσικό ιδίωμα της Κοζάνης* (2005), whose entry count was not found stated anywhere. No open, machine-readable Kozani lexicon exists: the only open sources are the partial TEI-XML digitisation of the Ακαδημία Αθηνών ΙΛΝΕ (letters Α to Ε, no Kozani-specific layer) and Wiktionary (CC BY-SA, no Kozani category found), plus a public-domain 1892 Velvento study on Anemi.
