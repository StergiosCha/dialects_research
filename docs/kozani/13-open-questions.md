## 13. Open questions and gaps

The largest gap is procedural: in this session no web page could be opened (the cloud environment's network policy denied every host except github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com and pypi.org), so every row in sections 1 to 10 that is not a GitHub repository rests on search-engine records and carries [unverified: page not opened]. The questions below are grouped by what blocks the tutor's design, and each names the section and the contact that can settle it.

### Verification and access

1. **Re-verify every non-GitHub row.** Open each cited page and confirm sizes, dates, licences and URLs. Requires a session whose environment allows the Greek institutional hosts (uowm.gr, academyofathens.gr, ekt.gr, upatras.gr, auth.gr, uoc.gr, kozlib.gr, greek-language.gr, mdpi.com, benjamins.com, archive.org, wikipedia.org and the local media domains) under Network access in the environment settings, or a broader access level.
2. **Does a PDF of Ντίνας 2005 exist online?** Sections 1, 3 and 6 disagree (section 11). Settle on users.uowm.gr/kdinas.
3. **Who holds the rights of the Ινστιτούτο Βιβλίου και Ανάγνωσης Κοζάνης?** Whether it still operates and whether its publications (Ντίνας 2005, Κουζιάκη Παπαγγέλη 2008) passed to the Δήμος or the Κοβεντάρειος library. Section 10.
4. **Death years for public-domain reasoning.** Μπουντώνας (1941 reported, unconfirmed), Παπασιώπης, Αλευράς, Ζ. Πιτένης, the 1892 and 1933 ΙΛΝΕ collectors. Sections 2, 3, 7.

### Grammar

5. **Entry count and structure of the Ντίνας glossary**, and whether volume 1 carries a texts appendix. Sections 1, 2, 3.
6. **Clitic doubling conditions** with accusative indirect objects in Kozani town (obligatory or optional, in which constructions). Read Anagnostopoulou, Mertyris and Sevdali 2022 and Dimitriadis 1999. Section 11, item 3.
7. **Stress after deletion.** No source found that treats stress shift and the resulting clusters for Kozani specifically. Μαργαρίτη-Ρόγκα 1985 does it for Σιάτιστα. Does Ντίνας 2005 state the rules? Section 1.
8. **Rates and conditioning of deletion.** Extract from Lengeris, Kainada and Topintzi 2016 and Topintzi and Baltazani 2012 the actual percentages by position and vowel, to set the tutor's "variable" thresholds. Section 1.
9. **Verb and noun paradigms.** No open source lists full Kozani paradigms. They must come from Ντίνας 2005 or be re-elicited. Section 1.
10. **The loanword layer.** No Kozani-specific study of Slavic, Albanian or Aromanian loans was found (Κυρανούδης covers Turkish loans in Northern Greek generally, Λιθοξόου aggregates without licence). Section 2.

### Register and variation

11. **Who still speaks the town idiom?** No survey of dialect use by age in Kozani exists. The register split (older dialect form versus levelled urban norm) rests on Pappas 2017, whose locality is unknown, and on Πλαδή for Λιτόχωρο. Sections 7, 8.
12. **Which features are town-only?** The Θαβώρης 1994 article and the Ντίνας isogloss chapter must be read to separate Kozani from Σιάτιστα, Βελβεντό, Αιανή and Γρεβενά. Σέρβια, Βόιο and Εορδαία are undocumented beyond ΚΕΝΔΙ manuscripts. Section 7.
13. **Is the GRDD Northern portion already a Kozani corpus?** Section 3 records, unverified, that it was drawn from Kozani and Grevena. If so the author already holds about 35,000 words of Kozani text and the provenance of each file should be written down. Sections 3, 9.

### Data and licences

14. **No Kozani text with an open licence.** The carnival texts are traditional but their compilations are copyrighted and the satirical songs have living authors. One agreement with ΟΑΠΝ Δήμου Κοζάνης could cover the municipal material. Section 3.
15. **No Kozani audio with an open licence, and no confirmed Kozani holding in any institutional sound archive.** Ask ΚΕΝΔΙ, the Κέντρον Ερεύνης της Ελληνικής Λαογραφίας and the Μερλιέ archive for Kozani finding aids. Section 4.
16. **Will Κοζανιτολόγιο volumes 2 and 3 appear as free files**, and under what terms? Section 2.
17. **ΙΛΝΕ TEI licence and Kozani sigla.** Whether the digitised letters Α to Ε can be filtered by locality and reused. Section 2.
18. **Orthography.** No explicit convention exists. The tutor's provisional rule (section 6) must be checked against Ντίνας's transcription note and against the carnival site's practice once opened.

### Pedagogy and tools

19. **Who are the learners?** No teaching material exists, so there is no evidence of demand beyond the 2024 ημερίδα and the 2018 school feature. The author should decide whether the tutor targets locals, heritage speakers abroad, or linguists, since that sets the register and the exercise types. Section 5.
20. **No spelling-error or acquisition study for Northern children**, so error anticipation must be derived from the vowel rules themselves. Section 8.
21. **Kozani is not a class in any dialect identifier**, and no SMG to Northern converter exists. The transducer over the open SMG stack (section 12, item 6) is new work. Section 9.
22. **No ASR or TTS for Northern pronunciation.** A Kozani test set would need new recordings. Section 9.
