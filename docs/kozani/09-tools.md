## 9. Existing tools

No tool treats Kozani separately: the only dialect identifiers that see Northern Greek at all (the GRDD baselines and the DI_detector Flask app) use a single "Northern Greek" class with no regional metadata, no rule-based or finite-state converter for Northern vowel raising and deletion exists on GitHub, and no Kozani translator, quiz or keyboard was found in Greek or English searches. The most useful open base is the SMG stack: the eellak morphological dictionary (about 900,000 entries from Greek Wiktionary, CC BY-SA 3.0) plus the modern-greek-inflexion paradigm generator (MIT) to produce SMG forms, the apertium-ell monolingual dictionary (GPL-3.0) as a finite-state host for the vowel rules, and the Pattichis and Armostis Cypriot find-and-replace normaliser as the architectural model for an SMG to Kozani converter. The sandbox network policy refused every host except github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com and pypi.org, so arXiv, ACL Anthology, Hugging Face, Zenodo, clarin:el and greek-language.gr pages are marked [unverified: page not opened] and their facts come from the GitHub READMEs that cite them or from search summaries.
