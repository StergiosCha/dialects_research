## 7. Variation

The literature that places Kozani town within Western Macedonia is small but real: one 1994 Μακεδονικά survey of the nomos as Northern (the author recorded in the journal index is Α. Ι. Θαβώρης, not Κ. Ντίνας, to confirm), Ντίνας 2005 with isoglosses across Kozani and Grevena, three acoustic studies on Kozani speakers (Topintzi and Baltazani 2012, Lengeris, Kainada and Topintzi 2016, Lengeris et al. 2015), one sociolinguistic chapter (Pappas 2017), and single-locality phonologies for Αιανή, Σιάτιστα, Βελβεντό, Βέντζια Γρεβενών and Καστοριά.
