## 12. Ranked top ten items for the tutor

Ranked by how much of the tutor each item unlocks, weighted by how easily it can be licensed. Three of the ten are usable today under an open licence (items 6, 7 and 8), six need one permission request each, and one (item 9) needs an archive visit. Every row of the source tables is still marked [unverified: page not opened], so each item's first action is to open the cited page and confirm the facts recorded here.

1. **Ντίνας 2005, Το γλωσσικό ιδίωμα της Κοζάνης (sections 1, 2, 6).** Reuse: Ask. The only full grammar and the only scholarly glossary of the town idiom, two volumes, about 240 and 592 pages. Its rules and paradigms can be coded from the facts without permission, its tables, examples and glossary entries cannot. Without it the tutor's morphology rests on three phonetics papers and one syntax paper. First action: obtain a copy, confirm whether the author's page offers a PDF, write to Κ. Ντίνας (Πανεπιστήμιο Δυτικής Μακεδονίας) and to the rights holder of the Ινστιτούτο Βιβλίου και Ανάγνωσης Κοζάνης for permission to digitise the glossary and reuse example sentences.

2. **Lengeris, Kainada and Topintzi 2016, with Topintzi and Baltazani 2012 (sections 1, 7).** Reuse: Ask (facts free, open PDFs reported). The only quantitative statements of the two Kozani vowel rules: raising and deletion frequent but not categorical, in all unstressed positions, /u/ deleting more than /i/, diphthongisation rare, Kozani full Northern. These define how the tutor codes raising (near-obligatory) and deletion (variable). First action: open the Pasithee and eclass PDFs, extract the conditioning factors and rates into the rule specification.

3. **Anagnostopoulou, Mertyris and Sevdali 2022, Languages 7(3) (section 1).** Reuse: Open (CC BY expected for MDPI Languages, not confirmed). Fieldwork in Grevena and Kozani, states the accusative indirect object in every former dative domain and the clitic doubling conditions. The one syntax source for the exact area, and the only candidate for an openly licensed grammar paper. First action: open the MDPI page, confirm the licence, code the case rule, read the doubling conditions (section 11, item 3).

4. **Χριστοδούλου 2017, Κοζανιτολόγιο (section 2).** Reuse: Ask. About 6,600 lemmas of the living town idiom with cultural notes, the largest Kozani lexicon, and volumes 2 (SMG to Kozani) and 3 (description) announced as free electronic releases. If the author shares files, this becomes the tutor's lookup lexicon. First action: write to the author through the Σύνδεσμος Γραμμάτων και Τεχνών Ν. Κοζάνης, ask about the electronic volumes and a data licence.

5. **The carnival (φανοί) song corpus (sections 3, 4, 6).** Reuse: Ask (traditional texts, modern compilations and satirical songs copyrighted). The web list on kozanitikiapokria.gr, the Λάκκας 2010 volume, the Φτάκα-Τσικριτζή collection of 180 songs and the ΟΑΠΝ 2021 non-commercial CD together form the only sizeable body of Kozani dialect text with matching audio. The municipality controls most of it, so one agreement could cover text and recordings. First action: write to ΟΑΠΝ Δήμου Κοζάνης and Φ. Φτάκα-Τσικριτζή, ask for the song texts with their transcription convention and the CD audio under a reuse licence.

6. **The open SMG stack: eellak morphological dictionary, modern-greek-inflexion, apertium-ell (section 9).** Reuse: Open (CC BY-SA 3.0, MIT, GPL-3.0). About 900,000 morphological entries and a paradigm generator for SMG, plus a finite-state dictionary that can host a Northern transducer. Lets the tutor generate every SMG paradigm now and derive Kozani forms by rule, then diff the two for exercises. Implementable today. First action: build the raising and deletion transducer from items 1 and 2 and run it over the generator's output, keeping the GPL and share-alike terms separate.

7. **UD_Greek-Lesbian treebank and its README (section 6).** Reuse: Open (CC BY-SA 4.0). The only Northern Greek corpus with an explicit written policy for dialect spelling: apostrophe for deleted /i/ and /u/, phonetic ι and ου for raised vowels, SMG lemma underneath. Gives the tutor its display convention and its two-layer data model, and 625 sentences of a sister Northern variety for testing the transducer. Implementable today. First action: adopt the convention provisionally, revisit after reading Ντίνας's transcription note.

8. **Μπουντώνας 1892, Μελέτη περί του γλωσσικού ιδιώματος Βελβεντού (sections 1, 2, 7).** Reuse: PD (author reported to have died 1941, to confirm). The only public-domain grammar in the nomos, scanned on Ανέμη. Its paradigms and word material can be loaded outright for the Βελβεντό regional layer and as a 19th-century baseline for Kozani forms. First action: confirm the death year, download the Ανέμη scan, OCR the paradigms.

9. **ΚΕΝΔΙ-ΙΛΝΕ archive, Ακαδημία Αθηνών (sections 2, 3, 4, 7).** Reuse: Ask. Manuscript collections from Kozani dated 1892, 1933 and 1959, nomos collections from 1980 and 1983, Σιάτιστα, Βόιο and Βελβεντό glossaries, a sound archive of several hundred hours whose Kozani content is unconfirmed, and a partial TEI digitisation of the printed dictionary. The only place where authentic older Kozani speech and unpublished word lists may survive. First action: write to the Centre with the archive numbers reported in section 7 (ΙΛ 1199, ΙΛ 1243) and ask for a Kozani finding aid.

10. **The ΠΔΜ thesis Διδακτική αξιοποίηση του Κοζανίτικου ιδιώματος and Μαρωνίτη, Στάμου, Γρίβα and Ντίνας 2016 (section 8).** Reuse: Ask. The thesis is the only item that designs classroom exercises on Kozani forms for Gymnasium pupils, the 2016 chapter is the only dialect-awareness intervention run with Western Macedonian children, with pre and post evaluation. Together they give the tutor its exercise types and its awareness module. First action: open the dspace.uowm.gr record, read the activities, ask the authors to reuse the evaluation items.

Items that just missed the ten: Θαβώρης 1994 (open PDF, the nomos-wide rule statement for the variation layer), Πιτένης 2022 Γιαλάν ντουνιάς (the largest recent body of written Kozani dialogue, copyrighted), the ILSP greek-dialects-asr recipe (rerunnable on Kozani recordings once any exist), Common Voice Greek (CC0, SMG baseline only), and the Pattichis and Armostis OrthographicNormalizer (architectural model for the converter, no licence).
