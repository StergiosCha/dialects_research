## 11. Disagreements between sources

The Kozani literature is too small for many head-on disputes, but the filled tables show six disagreements on linguistic facts that change what the tutor codes, and a longer list of bibliographic discrepancies that a verification pass must settle before any row is cited. Every item below is drawn from sections 1 to 8, and since no page could be opened in this session the disagreements themselves are between search-engine records of the sources, not between the opened texts.

### Disagreements on linguistic facts

1. **Categorical or gradient vowel rules.** The classificatory tradition (Trudgill 2003 as summarised in section 7, Κοντοσόπουλος 2001, Θαβώρης 1994) states the two Northern rules as categorical grades: extreme Northern deletes every unstressed /i/ and /u/ and raises every unstressed /e/ and /o/, Northern deletes only word-final high vowels, semi-Northern only deletes. The acoustic studies on Kozani speakers (Topintzi and Baltazani 2012, Lengeris, Kainada and Topintzi 2016, section 1) find deletion and raising frequent but not categorical, not restricted to word-final position, and /u/ deleting more readily than /i/. Θαβώρης 1994 adds that deletion is "applied less strictly" than raising in the nomos. Consequence: the tutor should code raising as the near-obligatory rule and deletion as a frequent, variable rule with position and vowel quality as conditioning factors, and should accept undeleted forms as correct variants rather than errors.

2. **Where Kozani sits on the Northern scale.** Lengeris, Kainada and Topintzi 2016 place Kozani in the Northern, not semi-Northern, group, and the Trudgill summary calls Kozani "unambiguously northern". Κοντοσόπουλος lists neighbouring Καστοριά as semi-Northern, and Papadamou and Papanastassiou 2012 place Καστοριά by Τζιτζιλής's 24 isoglosses while noting a /sk/ development shared with Kozani and Grevena. Consequence: regional notes should mark Καστοριά forms without raising as a different grade, and the Kozani town variety as full Northern, pending the opened papers.

3. **Clitic doubling with accusative indirect objects.** Dimitriadis 1999 (section 1) is recorded as treating doubling of the accusative indirect object as obligatory in Macedonian Greek, with the clitic as case mediator. Anagnostopoulou, Mertyris and Sevdali 2022, based on 2019 fieldwork in Grevena and Kozani, are recorded as using the obligatory versus optional status of doubling to separate Northern from Pontic. Which value holds for Kozani town, and in which constructions (ditransitives, passives, imperatives), is not visible in the records. Consequence: the indirect-object rule can be coded now (accusative in every former dative domain), the doubling rule must wait for the opened 2022 paper.

4. **How to write the deleted vowel.** The Lesbian treebank data (section 6) show that dictionary sources mark every deleted /i/ and /u/ with an apostrophe (51 to 53 of 54 example sentences), transcribed recordings and literary texts often leave the cluster bare (183 of 270 recorded sentences carry an apostrophe, literary forms such as τ μέρα, κτσιά), and the Kozani carnival site spells a category name as μασκαραλίτκα with a bare cluster. Kiparsky's working paper is recorded as marking the reduced vowel as a nonsyllabic i with a diacritic, and the scholarly layer (ΚΕΝΔΙ, ΙΛΝΕ) uses IPA or a Greek alphabet with added symbols. Consequence: the tutor needs a declared display convention. The evidence favours apostrophe for every deleted high vowel, phonetic ι and ου for raised vowels, plain τσ and τζ, with the SMG form as the lemma, following the treebank's two-layer design. Ντίνας 2005 may prescribe otherwise and must be checked first.

5. **The 3sg mediopassive ending.** The brief asked whether forms like έρχιτι appear as έρχιτ'. The treebank data show -ιτι with no apostrophe (έρχιτι, κάθιτι, φαίνιτι) in all 625 sentences and no έρχιτ' at all, because the final vowel is a raised /e/, not a deleted /i/. This agrees with the rule in item 1 but disagrees with the brief's assumption. Consequence: the display rule is "apostrophe only where an underlying /i/ or /u/ is deleted, ι where an underlying /e/ surfaces as /i/", and the paradigm generator must know the underlying vowel of each ending.

6. **Who taught dialect, and when.** The ΙΕΠ 2021 Γυμνάσιο curriculum (section 5) names geographical varieties as an aim, while the Patras student survey (section 8) states that only the 2011 Νέο Πρόγραμμα Σπουδών explicitly asked for dialect teaching. Παπαζαχαρίου et al. 2016 report that pupils judge dialect in formal settings as wrong speech, while the Ρέθυμνο intervention reports significantly positive pupil attitudes to dialect lessons. Consequence: the tutor cannot assume Kozani learners have met the Northern vowel rules at school, and should expect initial stigma that an awareness module can shift.

### Bibliographic discrepancies to settle on the opened pages

- **Authorship of the 1994 Μακεδονικά article.** HANDOVER.md attributes "Τα γλωσσικά ιδιώματα του νομού Κοζάνης ως βόρεια και οι κυριότερες ιδιορρυθμίες τους" to Κ. Ντίνας. The journal index (sections 1, 2, 7) lists Α. Ι. Θαβώρης, Μακεδονικά 29, 295 to 307. The tables follow the index.
- **A PDF of Ντίνας 2005.** Section 3 recorded a PDF URL under users.uowm.gr/kdinas, section 6 recorded the author page as "listing a PDF", section 1 found no PDF on any site. All three are unverified. If the PDF exists, the Access column of sections 1, 2, 3 and 6 changes.
- **Ντίνας 2005 page counts and volumes.** Vol. 1 is given as 237 or 240 pages, vol. 2 as 591 or 592, and one bookseller record lists a single 237-page volume. Biblionet titleid 110805 is the record to open.
- **Entry counts.** Κοζανιτολόγιο 2017 is given as about 6,600 or over 6,400 lemmas. The Ντίνας glossary has no stated count except one snippet saying around 5,000. The ΙΛΝΕ TEI set is given as 31,451 entries in one snippet.
- **Dates of local glossaries.** Χριστοδούλου's first dictionary is dated 2003 or 2005. Χατσιούλης's Siatista dictionary is dated 2004 or 2002. Ντίνας and Ζαρκογιάννη 2009 has different page counts in different bookseller records.
- **Page ranges.** Ανδριώτης 1933 is cited as 304 to 323 or 340 to 352. Topintzi and Baltazani 2012 as 369 to 398 or 373 to 402. Κατσάνης 1985 as 47 to 54 or 47 to 63. Trudgill 2003 as 45 to 64 or 45 to 63.
- **Institutional figures.** The Κοβεντάρειος library is credited with 1,550,000 digitised pages on one page and 400,000 digital documents on another. The Μερλιέ archive holds 584 or 600 songs. West Channel was founded in 1988 or 1991.
- **Death years.** Μπουντώνας is given as died 1941 in section 1 and as death year not confirmed in sections 2 and 7. The PD status of the only candidate public-domain grammar depends on it. Death years for Παπασιώπης, Αλευράς and Ζ. Πιτένης were not found at all.
- **Locality of Pappas 2017.** The one sociolinguistic study of raising and deletion as variables does not reveal in its record whether Kozani or another Northern town was sampled.

Not found: any published disagreement between Ντίνας 2005 and a later author on a Kozani grammatical fact, any review of Ντίνας 2005 or of Κοζανιτολόγιο that disputes an analysis, and any debate on the Kozani orthography in print.
