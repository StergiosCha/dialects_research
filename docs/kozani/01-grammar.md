## 1. Grammatical descriptions

The only full grammar of the town idiom is Ντίνας 2005 (two print volumes, no PDF located), and the explicit, measured rules for vowel deletion and raising come from three acoustic papers on Kozani (Topintzi and Baltazani 2012, Lengeris, Kainada and Topintzi 2016) and one open-access syntax paper on the accusative indirect object (Anagnostopoulou, Mertyris and Sevdali 2022), so a tutor grammar can be built from facts in these but every paradigm table must be re-elicited.
