## 4. Audio and speech

No openly licensed recording of Kozani speech was found: the audio that exists is either broadcaster or association copyright (ΕΡΤ, local CDs, YouTube uploads) or sits in institutional sound archives (ΙΛΝΕ, ΚΕΕΛ, Μουσικό Λαογραφικό Αρχείο) whose Kozani holdings could not be confirmed, and no Northern Greek ASR or TTS resource exists.
