## 3. Text corpora and texts

No Kozani text collection with an explicit open licence was found: the carnival (φανοί) song texts exist as a web list, a 2010 compiled volume and a 180 song collection, and the dialect prose and poetry (1964 to 2022) is all in copyright, while a PDF of Ντίνας 2005 is reported on the author's university page but was not confirmed (section 1 found no PDF of it). The sandbox network policy refused every host except GitHub, so no page was opened and every row carries [unverified: page not opened], and the GRDD paper (arXiv 2308.00802) is reported in search results as stating that its Northern Greek portion comes from Kozani and Grevena only (about 35k words), with GRDD+ listing 33,292 Northern words after adding Lesbos, Samothrace and Thrace.
