## 8. Pedagogy and acquisition

The pedagogy literature that names Kozani or Western Macedonia is thin and sits almost entirely in the Πανεπιστήμιο Δυτικής Μακεδονίας (Φλώρινα) circle around Κωνσταντίνος Ντίνας: a didactic-use book on dialects, a critical-literacy intervention with grade 1 and 2 pupils on televised dialect, a postgraduate course on γλωσσική ποικιλότητα and a repository thesis proposing Gymnasium activities built on the Kozani idiom, while attitude work on Northern varieties exists for Thessaly and Λιτόχωρο rather than Kozani and no study of Northern children's spelling or phonological acquisition was found.
