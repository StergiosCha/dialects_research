## 5. Teaching materials and curricula

No structured course, textbook, app or lesson set for learning Cretan was found: the only formal teaching is one university elective at the Παιδαγωγικό Τμήμα Προσχολικής Εκπαίδευσης in Ρέθυμνο (from the spring semester of 2019 to 2020), plus a dialectology survey course at the Τμήμα Φιλολογίας. The national curriculum treats dialects as a language-awareness topic (encouraged by the 2003 ΔΕΠΠΣ, required by the 2011 Πρόγραμμα Σπουδών), textbooks give geographic varieties little space, and Cretan enters school only through the Renaissance texts of the Κείμενα Νεοελληνικής Λογοτεχνίας anthologies. Everything else found is popular web glossaries and association pages, none licensed for reuse.
