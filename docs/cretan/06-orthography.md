## 6. Orthography: proposals and conventions

No source found states a complete spelling convention for Cretan: practice converges on the historical orthography of Standard Modern Greek with the digraphs κι, τσ, τζ for the palatal and affricate outputs, an apostrophe for elided vowels, no diacritics on κ, λ or σ, and a split between ίντα (etymological, argued for in the Heraklion press) and είντα (the majority spelling in the texts GRDD already holds), so the explicit models are Cypriot (Κατσογιάννου, Αρμοστής and colleagues), and the only full Cretan convention available is the implicit one that can be read off the GRDD files. No row below earns the **Implementable.** prefix, the GRDD counts are the nearest thing to a rule set.
