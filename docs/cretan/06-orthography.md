## 6. Orthography: proposals and conventions

No source states a complete spelling convention for Cretan: practice converges on the historical orthography of Standard Modern Greek with the digraphs κι, τσ, τζ for the palatal and affricate outputs, an apostrophe for elided vowels, no diacritics on κ, λ or σ, μπ and ντ for the prenasalised stops, and an open three-way dispute over ίντα (etymological, Πατρίς, Βικιλεξικό), είντα (the majority in the literary texts GRDD holds) and ήντα (defended by cretans.gr). The only explicit conventions found are transcription policies rather than orthographies (ΚΕΝΔΙ's IPA table, the VarDial 2026 ASR benchmark's "SMG-based orthography preserving dialectal forms"), the only complete codification proposals are Cypriot (Αρμοστής, Κατσογιάννου and colleagues, Papadima), and the fullest implicit Cretan convention remains the one read off the GRDD files, so no row below earns the **Implementable.** prefix for spelling, one earns it for the IPA layer.
