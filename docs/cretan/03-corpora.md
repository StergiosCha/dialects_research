## 3. Text corpora and texts

The public-domain, directly loadable Cretan text is the Renaissance corpus: the Βικιθήκη transcriptions of Ερωτόκριτος (five parts) and Ερωφίλη, and the Anemi scans of the pre-1926 folk-song and mantinada collections of Κριάρης (1920) and Βλαστός (1893), all of whose authors died before 1956. The largest modern-dialect sources are mantinada collections (mantinades.gr with about 21,000 user-contributed couplets, Ιδομενέως with about 12,000 in print) and the folklore periodicals (Κρητικά Χρονικά, partly open online), all copyrighted or without stated terms, so they need permission. The GRDD Cretan part already in hand was built, per its GitHub README, from web text, blogs, websites, theatrical plays and Σφακιανάκης's Cretan translations of ancient tragedies and comedies, so those sources should not be recollected.
