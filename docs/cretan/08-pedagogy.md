## 8. Pedagogy and acquisition

The Cretan dialect in education is studied almost entirely by Μαρίνα Τζακώστα and her coauthors at the Παιδαγωγικό Τμήμα Προσχολικής Εκπαίδευσης of the Πανεπιστήμιο Κρήτης (Ρέθυμνο), framed by the general Greek dialect-in-school literature (Ντίνας and Ζαρκογιάννη, Αρχάκης and Τσάκωνα) and by the Cypriot intervention and attitude studies, which remain the only Greek bidialectal work with explicit protocols and instruments.
