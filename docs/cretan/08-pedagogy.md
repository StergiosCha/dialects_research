## 8. Pedagogy and acquisition

Cretan in education is a small literature centred on Μαρίνα Τζακώστα (ΠΤΠΕ, Πανεπιστήμιο Κρήτης, Ρέθυμνο) and her students: attitude surveys of teachers, pupils and university students (teachers accept oral Cretan but correct it in writing, pupils and students are more favourable than teachers), one documented classroom intervention built on mantinades, and a cluster of ΕΑΠ and ΑΠΚυ master's theses, with no published competence test, no perceptual prestige ranking of Cretan among Greek accents, no vitality assessment and no study of foreign learners acquiring Cretan. The Cypriot bidialectal work remains the only Greek source of explicit intervention protocols.
