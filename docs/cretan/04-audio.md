## 4. Audio and speech

Transcribed, aligned Cretan speech that can be loaded directly exists in one place: the ILSP (ΕΚ Αθηνά) Cretan speech corpora on Hugging Face, about 2 hours of eastern Cretan radio narrative (CC BY 4.0) plus a 2026 extension with western material and two Cretan ASR models. Everything else is either untranscribed sound held by institutions (ΚΕΝΔΙ, ΚΕΕΛ, Μουσείο Κρητικής Εθνολογίας, Harvard Notopoulos tapes), broadcast material under paid or unclear licences (ΕΡΤ, Κρήτη TV), or oral history with transcripts but no reuse right (Istorima).
