## 12. Ranked top ten items for the tutor

Ranked by what the tutor can do with the item now, weighted by reuse status: open data that already contains Cretan comes first, then facts that can be coded without permission, then the licences worth asking for. Three of the ten are openly licensed and loadable today, four supply rules that can be coded from facts, and three are permission requests. Columns: rank, item, section, reuse, reason, first action.

| Rank | Item | Section | Reuse | Reason | First action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ILSP Cretan speech corpus (about 2 h eastern Cretan radio narrative, transcribed and aligned) with the 2026 extension (about 4 h with western speech) and the xls-r-greek-cretan ASR model | 4, 9 | Open (CC BY 4.0 on the base card, extension and model licences unverified) | The only transcribed Cretan speech and the only Cretan recogniser (28.27 percent word error rate), the base for listening exercises and pronunciation feedback | Open the cards, confirm the licences with ILSP, ask about the 23 undigitised Radio Mires tapes at the Βικελαία |
| 2 | UD_Greek-Cretan treebank with the open-greek dilemma lemmatizer and parser | 2, 9 | Open (CC BY-SA 4.0, MIT) | 1,009 lemmas with manual morphological features and a parser already trained on Cretan, so the tutor can lemmatise and check learner input without asking anyone | Run dilemma on the GRDD Cretan file and measure coverage against the treebank |
| 3 | Πάγκαλος vol. 1 (1955, Academy reissue) with Χαιρετάκης 2020 on Cretan inflection and derivation | 1, 2 | Facts free, texts Ask | The only full paradigm set and the only modern inflection class system, together the backbone of the morphology module | Ask Χαιρετάκης at ΚΕΝΔΙ for his inflection tables, confirm whether Anemi serves Πάγκαλος vol. 1 |
| 4 | Κοντοσόπουλος 1988, Γλωσσικός Άτλας της Κρήτης (163 settlements, IPA), with his east vs west feature list | 1, 7 | Facts free, maps Ask | The only per-village evidence for the regional layers, needed because GRDD and GRDD+ carry no regional labels and palatalisation is not island-wide | Buy the 4th edition (ΠΕΚ 2016) and encode one variable per map with east, west and Σφακιά values |
| 5 | University of Crete phonetics (Lengeris and Kappa 2016, Lengeris et al. 2014, Kappa, Tzakosta) with the ΚΕΝΔΙ IPA table | 1, 6, 7 | Facts free, ΚΕΝΔΙ table open to adopt | Codable pronunciation rules with stated environments (stress, word position, back vowels) and a complete transcription convention for the IPA layer | Ask Kappa what recordings and word lists the 2012 to 2014 western Cretan project left |
| 6 | Public-domain text: Ερωτόκριτος and Ερωφίλη (Βικιθήκη), Κριάρης 1920 and Βλαστός 1893 (Anemi), Λιουδάκη 1933 (PD since 2018) | 3 | PD | Loadable reading text and the historical model of the mantinada at no cost beyond polytonic OCR and spelling normalisation | Download the Anemi scans, check the source edition of the Βικιθήκη Ερωτόκριτος is not Αλεξίου's |
| 7 | GRDD spelling counts with the rule_dialnorm Cretan to Standard Greek map | 6 | Facts free (GRDD own data), rule_dialnorm Ask (no licence file) | The only quantitative evidence for each spelling choice and the only explicit Cretan spelling rules found, enough to fix the tutor's display orthography and its accepted variants | Decide ίντα vs είντα vs ήντα (section 11) and extend the rule map to τση, τσι, τζη, εδά and the augment |
| 8 | The author's GRDD tool set: dialect classifiers and DI_detector, krikri_dialectal adapters, Dialect_Trans, Greek_Rhyme | 9 | Own work (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 or research only) | Already in hand: a Cretan detector, the only Greek dialect generation weights, the first Cretan to Standard Greek parallel data and a rhyme verifier for a mantinada track | Settle the licence for a commercial tutor, evaluate the adapters on Cretan prompts, collect Dialect_Trans renderings |
| 9 | Ξανθινάκης (west, ΠΕΚ 2023) and Πιτυκάκης (east, 1983), with Κοντοσόπουλος's Αντίστροφο λεξικό (Standard Greek to Cretan) | 2, 10 | Ask (ΠΕΚ, Ίδρυμα Πιτυκάκη, Βιβλιοεπιλογή) | No open lexicon beyond 273 Wiktionary headwords exists, a licensed dictionary is the only route to a real lookup and production lexicon | Request a non-exclusive educational licence from ΠΕΚ with Χαραλαμπάκης's support, start with the 180-page reverse dictionary |
| 10 | Τζακώστα's Cretan pedagogy: the ΠΤΠΕ elective syllabus, the Καψάσκη mantinada lesson protocol, the ΨηΜοΠΙ pupil-collected recordings | 5, 8 | Ask | The only Cretan-specific curriculum, the one documented classroom intervention and attitude evidence from Crete, which together set the lesson sequence and the register stance (oral Cretan welcomed, written Cretan unsettled) | Ask Τζακώστα for the syllabus, the course materials and the terms of the ΨηΜοΠΙ recordings |

Not found: an openly licensed Cretan dictionary of any size, a Cretan text to speech voice, a published Cretan learner course or textbook, and a mantinada corpus with a reuse licence, any of which would have entered the top ten.
