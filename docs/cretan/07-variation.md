## 7. Variation: atlases, regional and sociolinguistic studies, diaspora

The east vs west layering of the tutor rests on two printed sources, Κοντοσόπουλος's geolinguistic study of Crete and his dialect handbook, with Trudgill 2003 as the English-language classification that confirms Cretan's place among the southern dialects, and the two regional lexica (Πιτυκάκης for the east, Ξανθινάκης for the west) as the only sources whose scope labels forms by region. No sociolinguistic study of levelling, attitudes or urban Cretan, no phonetic study of regional realisations and no linguistic study of the Muslim diaspora in Turkey, Syria, Lebanon or Libya was confirmed.
