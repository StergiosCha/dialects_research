## 7. Variation: atlases, regional and sociolinguistic studies, diaspora

The east vs west and Sfakian layers of the tutor can rest on published evidence: Κοντοσόπουλος's atlas (163 settlements, IPA transcription) and his thesis, Πάγκαλος, and a chain of regional lexica (Ξανθινάκης for the west, Πιτυκάκης for Μεραμπέλλο, Κασσωτάκης for the Λασίθι plateau, Γαρεφαλάκης for Σητεία, Ξενάκη-Ροβίθη for Ηράκλειο, Περιστεράκης and Γερωνυμάκης for Σφακιά), with University of Crete acoustic work on western palatalisation and retroflex /l/. Sociolinguistic work on Cretan is about attitudes and school use rather than levelling, no perceptual dialectology of Greece placing Cretan was found, and the only linguistic study of the Muslim diaspora is the Cunda paper by Ioannidou, Ralli, Neokleous and Andreou.
