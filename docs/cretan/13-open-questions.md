## 13. Open questions and gaps

The largest gaps are in syntax (no study of clitic placement, negation or word order in contemporary spoken Cretan), in the urban register the scope section treats as the levelled end of the tutor's register axis (no study of urban Ηράκλειο, Χανιά or Ρέθυμνο speech), and in reusable data (no open dictionary, no licensed mantinada corpus, no Cretan text to speech). The questions below are ordered by how much of the tutor's design waits on them. Columns: question, why it matters, where the answer may be, who to ask, sections, and whether the tutor's design is blocked until it is answered.
