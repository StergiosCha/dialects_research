## 5. Teaching materials and curricula

Pontic is taught almost entirely outside the state system: one university elective (ΔΠΘ, since 2018), a discontinued 100 hour university course (ΠΑΜΑΚ, four years to 2019 or 2020), and a dense network of free association and diaspora courses, most of them taught by a small circle of certified instructors (Μαχαιρίδου, Ιωακειμίδου, Κωνσταντινίδου) who draw on two printed course books, the Πανελλήνιος Σύνδεσμος Ποντίων Εκπαιδευτικών adult manual (Κυριακίδης, 2nd ed. 2018) and the Χατζόπουλος and Ιωακειμίδου 31 lesson beginners' method (Μαλλιάρης Παιδεία, 2022). No dedicated Pontic learning app, Memrise or Anki deck, or Duolingo course was confirmed, and the search budget ran out before the app store, diaspora (Germany, Sydney), ΠΟΕ seminar and Romeyka course queries could be completed, so those remain open.
