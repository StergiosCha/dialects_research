## 12. Ranked top ten items for the tutor

Ranked by how much each item advances the tutor per unit of effort and risk, taking into account that almost nothing for the koine is both open and machine-readable. "Now" means loadable without asking anyone, "Ask" means one request letter stands between the item and the tutor. Section numbers point to the rows with the details.

| Rank | Item | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ILSP Athena RC Pontic speech corpus with time-aligned Greek-script transcription and ASR baseline (Konstantinidou, Tsoukala, Stamou, Giouli, Markantonatou 2026), with the ΑΠΘ Pontic treebank in preparation (sections 3, 4) | The only resource that describes exactly the variety the tutor teaches, contemporary Pontic of Northern Greece, and its transcription scheme was chosen with the community, so it settles the orthography question for display. Audio plus transcript is the raw material for listening items, pronunciation feedback and example sentences. | Ask, no public release yet, the treebank shell announces CC BY 4.0 |
| 2 | Lingua Pontica online dictionary, Εύξεινος Λέσχη Βέροιας Τμήμα Νέων with Digital Terra, about 20,000 lemmas with pronunciation, source, examples, grammar notes and audio (section 2) | Largest structured Pontic lexicon in existence, already merged from six dictionaries including Αθανασιάδης 2020, with audio per entry. An export would give the tutor its lexicon backbone and a pronunciation reference in one step. Underlying sources are copyrighted, so the request must go through the society. | Ask (no terms published) |
| 3 | The two course books: Χατζόπουλος and Ιωακειμίδου 2022, 31 lessons for beginners (Μαλλιάρης Παιδεία), and the Πανελλήνιος Σύνδεσμος Ποντίων Εκπαιδευτικών adult manual, 2nd ed. 2018 (section 5) | The only graded syllabi for the koine. Their lesson order, grammar progression and exercise types are facts and can structure the tutor's levels without permission. The texts need a licence. The Veria YouTube lessons with quizzes (Μαχαιρίδου) show the item types in use. | Print, structure usable now, texts Ask |
| 4 | Reference grammars: Παπαδόπουλος 1955 (Ιστορική γραμματική), Οικονομίδης 1958 (Γραμματική της ελληνικής διαλέκτου του Πόντου, author's text PD since 2009), Drettas 1997 (Aspects pontiques) with Janse's 2002 review as the English way in (section 1) | The paradigms the tutor must code exist nowhere else. Οικονομίδης' own text is out of copyright, so its tables can be keyed in and reused verbatim. Παπαδόπουλος and Drettas supply the rules (free to follow) but not the examples. Their different sub-dialect bases must be reconciled (section 11, item 1). | Print, Οικονομίδης PD, others Ask for examples |
| 5 | Open rule statements: Ρεβυθιάδου and Σπυρόπουλος 2009 Οφίτικα report (open PDF), Spyropoulos and Kakarikos 2009 on nominal features, Michelioudakis and Sitaridou 2016 on wh-fronting (CC BY), Kooij and Revithiadou 2002 on stress (section 1) | The only paradigm-level or condition-level statements that are downloadable today: stress, stop voicing, γ epenthesis, final ι deletion, the definite nominative -ον, plural syncretism, wh-fronting as focus movement. Enough to build the first phonology and nominal morphology modules, flagged Οφίτικα where the source is. | Open PDF, Open (CC BY) for Glossa |
| 6 | Orthography and input package: Παπαδόπουλος-style Greek script with combining diaeresis and caron, msologr-cpu Pontic Sans and Serif fonts (SIL OFL) and Keyman package, AricSemerji iOS and macOS layouts, krhkayra fcitx5 input method (GPL 3.0), plus the Pontic Wikipedia and Soviet mapping tables as input variants (section 6) | Fully implementable now: exact code points, fonts that render the combining stack, mobile and desktop input. The normalisation table (U+0308 and U+0324 to one form, εα and εο digraphs, Latin Romeyka) can be written from the rows as they stand. | Now, fonts Open (OFL), fcitx5 Open (GPL), Apple layouts no licence stated |
| 7 | Wiktionary Pontic material, Greek edition Κατηγορία:Ποντιακά and English edition Category:Pontic Greek lemmas (103 lemmas), extracted with wiktextract (MIT) from the dumps (section 2) | The only open machine-readable Pontic lexicon. Small, but structured (part of speech, etymology, inherited versus Turkish loan categories) and licensed CC BY-SA, so it is the seed lexicon and the test set for whatever Lingua Pontica or a scanned dictionary later supplies. | Now, Open (CC BY-SA) |
| 8 | Texts: Λιανίδης 1962, Τα παραμύθια του ποντιακού λαού (457 pages of Pontic tales with facing SMG translation) under licence from the ΕΠΜ, and the PD set that needs only scanning: Φωτιάδης' plays (died 1909), Παρχαρίδης' Of tales and proverbs (1884 to 1885), Dawkins' tales and 1937 survey (died 1955) (section 3) | Λιανίδης is a ready-made parallel corpus for reading and translation items. The PD items are small but free, and Φωτιάδης' dialogue is the nearest thing to conversational koine in print. | Ask (Λιανίδης), PD (the rest, print only) |
| 9 | Berikashvili and Skopeteas 2026, Pontic Greek in the Caucasus online corpus, 373 recordings, 7 h 26 min, 43,073 words, WAV plus ELAN XML with glosses, described as open access (sections 3, 4) | The only downloadable transcribed Pontic audio. Georgian Pontic, so not the model for the koine, but usable at once for listening items, pronunciation contrast and as a test bed for any speech processing before the ILSP data arrives. | Now (licence name to confirm) |
| 10 | Pronunciation module sources: Armostis, Voniati, Drosos and Tafiadis 2020, JIPA illustration of Τραπεζουντιακά in Etoloakarnania (inventory, post-tonic high vowel deletion, final ν retention), and the Παπαδόπουλος dictionary pronunciation rules summarised by pontos-news (sections 1, 4, 6) | Gives the phoneme inventory and the two or three segmental rules a pronunciation module needs, with the measured finding that the fronted vowels merge in Greece-based speakers, which decides what the tutor accepts as correct. | Paywalled, rules free to follow |

Near misses, worth one query each: the Pontic Wikipedia (681 articles, CC BY-SA, disputed quality) as orthographic noise data rather than model text, pontianlyrics.gr (10,156 songs with per-song glossaries) if its team agrees to a glossary export, Νικοπολιτίδης 2011 (Λεξικό της ποντιακής διαλέκτου, Κυριακίδης, in commerce) as the commercial lexicon to license if Lingua Pontica declines, the Sitaridou Romeyka corpus (over 29 GB) for the comparison module if access terms can be agreed, and the ΚΕΝΔΙ sound archive for first generation recordings by visit.
