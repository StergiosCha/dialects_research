## 13. Open questions and gaps

Grouped by who can close them: the author, a session with ordinary network access, or a request letter.

### For the author

1. **Holdings.** The scope assumed only GRDD and GRDD+ are in hand. If the author holds Παπαδόπουλος 1955 or 1958 to 1961, Οικονομίδης 1958, Drettas 1997, Τομπαΐδης 1988 or any association course book, sections 1, 2 and 5 change from "Print, Ask" to "in hand" for those rows and the top ten reorders.
2. **Which base for the koine.** Παπαδόπουλος and Drettas describe Χαλδιώτικα, Οικονομίδης and the JIPA illustration describe Τραπεζουντιακά (section 11, item 1). The tutor needs one default paradigm set. The scope file proposes the koine of the association courses as default, which in practice means whichever base the ΠΣΠΕ manual and the Χατζόπουλος method follow, and that was not established.
3. **Fronted vowels in the target pronunciation.** Display with diacritics and accept the merged pronunciation, or teach the fronted vowels as a target. The JIPA finding (section 4) supports the first, the dictionaries and courses presuppose the second.
4. **GRDD's Pontic portion.** Its size, source texts and orthographic convention were not examined here (in hand). Whether it is Wikipedia text, song lyrics or something else decides how much of sections 3 and 6 it already covers.
5. **Romeyka in version one.** The scope file keeps Romeyka as a comparison module. If the author wants it as a teaching target, the Sitaridou corpus access question (below) becomes the first request, and section 6 needs a Latin-script display convention.

### For a session with network access (verification pass)

6. **Every row marked "[unverified: page not opened]".** Open the link, confirm or correct sizes, years, licences and contact pages, delete the marker. The hosts the session could not reach are listed in the scope file. Sections 1 to 7 carry about 120 such rows.
7. **Section 7 rerun.** The variation section was built from Glottolog alone. It needs the Παπαδόπουλος classification confirmed from the grammar, the post-1923 koineisation literature, the Georgia and Caucasus studies, the diaspora studies and the UNESCO Atlas level, as its "Not found" line itemises.
8. **Sections 8, 9 and 10.** At the time of writing their reruns were still in progress. Their findings are to be folded into sections 11 and 12 (attitude and transmission studies into item 7 of section 11, any Pontic NLP model or dialect-ID result into the top ten) when they land.
9. **Entry counts and digitisation.** No source gave an entry count for the Παπαδόπουλος dictionary, and no digitised copy was confirmed on Anemi or archive.org. The same holds for the Νικοπολιτίδης and Τσοπουρίδης dictionaries. The Pontic Wikipedia article count (681) rests on a Meta list, the dump listing was unreachable.
10. **Death years that decide PD status.** Σ. Λιανίδης, Δ. Κ. Παπαδόπουλος, Κ. Τοπχαράς, G. Drettas (and whether living), Δ. Τομπαΐδης (and whether living), Ι. Παρχαρίδης (1858 to 1910 in one row, not found in another).
11. **Licences stated but not read.** Pontic Wikipedia and Wiktionary CC BY-SA (inferred from Wikimedia norms), the Göttingen corpus "open access" (licence name not given), the Neocleous thesis, the Frontiers 2025 and MDPI 2022 articles, the Sitaridou 2023 Keria paper (CC BY-SA per the repository record), the ERT archive terms (FAQ reported as about ten years old).
12. **Unrun queries from the section agents.** Each "Not found" line in sections 2 to 6 ends with the queries that the shared search budget cut off: app stores and Memrise or Anki decks for Pontic (section 5), Drettas' and Sitaridou's transcription systems and Ömer Asan's alphabet (section 6), kaikki.org Greek-edition extraction and Pontic to English dictionaries (section 2), Zenodo and ELAR deposits and the Sitaridou corpus location (section 4), the Αρχείον Πόντου digitisation status and Wikisource page list (section 3).

14. **Εύξεινος Λέσχη Βέροιας, Τμήμα Νέων, and Digital Terra**: export of Lingua Pontica (about 20,000 lemmas with audio) and the rights position of the six source dictionaries it merges.
15. **Επιτροπή Ποντιακών Μελετών**: licence for Λιανίδης 1962 and for example material from the Παπαδόπουλος grammar and dictionary, and whether a scan of either exists internally.
16. **Πανελλήνιος Σύνδεσμος Ποντίων Εκπαιδευτικών and Μαλλιάρης Παιδεία**: the course books' texts and exercises, and what the instructor certification tests, which would give the tutor its level descriptors.
17. **Ioanna Sitaridou**: access terms for the Romeyka recordings and texts for a comparison module, and the deposit location.
18. **ΚΕΝΔΙ, Ακαδημία Αθηνών**: the list of Pontic holdings in the sound archive and the manuscript glossaries, and the terms for digitising a sample.
19. **Ιστορικό Αρχείο Προσφυγικού Ελληνισμού, Καλαμαριά**: what share of its 2,000 to 2,500 testimonies is in Pontic and whether any audio is released.
20. **Svetlana Berikashvili and Stavros Skopeteas**: the licence of the Göttingen corpus and whether any sessions were recorded in Greece.

### Gaps with no owner yet

21. **No Pontic ASR or TTS** exists (section 4), and the ILSP baseline reports zero-shot recognition as still challenging. Pronunciation feedback will have to start from forced alignment on the tutor's own recorded prompts, not from recognition.
22. **No corpus frequencies for koine variation.** Clitic placement, the two genitives of n-stem nouns, the -ον definite nominative, the surviving or merged fronted vowels: no study gives rates for the Greece-based variety, so the tutor cannot yet grade a form as "acrolectal" or "regional" on evidence, only on the sub-dialect base of the source grammar.
23. **No Pontic child acquisition or bidialectal study** surfaced in sections 1 to 7 (section 8 will say whether any exists). Error anticipation for SMG-dominant learners therefore has no published basis beyond the course books' own grammar notes.
24. **No conversational text in machine-readable form.** Theatre (Φωτιάδης, Κτενίδης) is print only, the ILSP conversations are unreleased, the Wikipedia is expository. Dialogue material for the tutor will have to be written or licensed.
25. **No agreed orthography body.** Neither the ΕΠΜ nor the ΠΟΕ has published a binding convention (section 6). The tutor's display choice will itself be a statement, and should be labelled as "after Παπαδόπουλος, encoded as in the Pontic keyboard projects" rather than as a standard.
