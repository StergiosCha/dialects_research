## 4. Audio and speech

No Pontic ASR or TTS model and no licensed downloadable speech dataset for the Pontic koine of Greece was found: the only open access audio with time aligned transcriptions is the Göttingen corpus of Pontic spoken in Georgia (7 h 26 min), and the most relevant corpus for the tutor (ILSP 2026, natural conversation in Northern Greece, Greek script transcripts) has no public download yet, so it must be requested. Everything else is streamed, copyrighted or in physical archives.
