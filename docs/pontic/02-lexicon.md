## 2. Lexical resources

The largest lexicon remains the two volume Παπαδόπουλος dictionary of 1958 to 1961 (print only, no entry count found, no digitised copy confirmed), while the largest online lexicon is Lingua Pontica with about 20,000 entries (website, no reuse terms found). The only open, machine-readable Pontic lexical data confirmed is the Wiktionary material (English edition: 103 Pontic Greek lemmas, Greek edition: count not stated) obtainable through the MIT licensed wiktextract tool and its kaikki.org downloads.
