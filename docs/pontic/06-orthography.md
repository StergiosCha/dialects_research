## 6. Orthography: proposals and conventions

Four competing conventions are in circulation: the Παπαδόπουλος and ΕΠΜ dictionary system with special glyphs, the Topcharas-derived phonetic system of the Pontic Wikipedia, the 1926 Soviet 20-letter orthography (historical only), and Turkish-based Latin spelling for Romeyka, with no body (ΕΠΜ or ΠΟΕ) found to have published a binding statement. For display the tutor should adopt a Papadopoulos-style Greek script with combining diaeresis on α and ο and combining caron on σ ζ ξ ψ (the encoding the three GitHub keyboard projects already implement), and accept in input plain Greek without diacritics, Wikipedia-style εα εο digraphs, and Latin Romeyka spellings.
