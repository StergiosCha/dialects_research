## 7. Variation: atlases, regional and sociolinguistic studies, diaspora

The shared web search budget of this session was exhausted before any query for this section ran, so the table rests on the one reachable host, GitHub: the Glottolog languoid tree for Pontic (pont1253) and its curated bibliography hh.bib, both opened and both CC BY 4.0. They confirm the classical regional descriptions (Dawkins, Παπαδόπουλος, Τομπαΐδης, Drettas), the Romeyka studies in Turkey (Mackridge, Özkan, Bortone, Sitaridou, Schreiber, Brendemoen as contact background) and Berikashvili on Georgia, but no post 1923 koineisation study for Macedonia, no Ofitika project page, no Russian, Ukrainian or Armenian Pontic study and no Pontic specific diaspora study could be confirmed. Every row below except the first two needs the ordinary verification pass (open the item, confirm access and licence).
