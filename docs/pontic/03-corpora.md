## 3. Text corpora and texts

The largest machine-readable Pontic text sources outside GRDD are the Pontic Wikipedia (681 articles, Wikipedia's CC BY-SA terms, small and of disputed quality) and the song-lyrics database pontianlyrics.gr (10,156 songs, copyrighted, no licence). Public-domain Pontic prose is confined to pre-1956 authors: Παρχαρίδης (1880s), Φωτιάδης (died 1909) and Dawkins (died 1955), while the big folk-tale, theatre and prose collections of Λιανίδης, Άκογλου, Κτενίδης and Μελανοφρύδης stay in copyright until 2032 to 2038 and exist only in print.
