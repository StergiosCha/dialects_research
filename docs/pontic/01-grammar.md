## 1. Grammatical descriptions

The koine of Greece has three reference grammars (Παπαδόπουλος 1955, Οικονομίδης 1958, Drettas 1997) and none of them is online, so the only openly downloadable paradigm-level descriptions found are for sub-dialects (Οφίτικα: Ρεβυθιάδου and Σπυρόπουλος 2009, Georgian Pontic: Berikashvili 2017) and for single phenomena (nominal features, wh-fronting, Romeyka infinitive and word order). Romeyka is far better served by open recent work than the koine is.
