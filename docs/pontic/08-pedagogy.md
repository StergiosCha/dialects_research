## 8. Pedagogy and acquisition

Pedagogical research on Pontic is thin and almost entirely sociolinguistic: a cluster of ΕΑΠ master's theses on attitudes of pupils, adolescents, teachers and adult learners, one large questionnaire survey of 259 speakers, one psycholinguistic study of Pontic and SMG bidialectals, and the Romeyka attitude and revitalisation work of Sitaridou's group. No child acquisition study, no validated proficiency or placement test and no evaluated learning app were found, so the only directly reusable instruments are a learner error inventory from a certified instructor, a translation test used in one thesis, and two attitude questionnaires.
