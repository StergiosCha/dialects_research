6. Orthography
No standard exists, but the Συντυχιές project (Katsoyannou, Armostis, Christodoulou, Themistocleous)
published the most explicit convention, which writes geminates and marks postalveolars, while current
ASR data (Common Voice el-CY) uses plain Greek letters without the breve, so the tutor needs a
normalisation table mapping both.

continuum and carrying growing prestige, unive frontiersin which supports koine as the tutor's default
register, with basilectal regional forms taught as marked (χωρκάτικα) options.
